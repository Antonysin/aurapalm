AuraPalm — 5 Design Directions

1. Celestial 2. Obsidian 3. Organic 4. Editorial 5. Neo-Future
Style 01 — Celestial Minimal

Theme

Light mode, warm off-white, generous whitespace. Like Calm meets Apple.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond (display) + Source Sans 3 (body) + Space Grotesk (UI)

Best For

Broadest appeal. Wellness seekers 30-55. Maximum trust & conversion.

Mood

Calm. Premium. Trustworthy. Opening a beautifully designed book.

Style 02 — Obsidian Luxury

Theme

True black obsidian with jewel tones. Cartier meets Mercury.

Typography

Playfair Display (display) + Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (data)

Best For

High-income demographics. Premium pricing justification. Ages 35-60.

Mood

Expensive. Confident. Timeless. A black-tie invitation.

Style 03 — Organic Ethereal

Theme

Earth tones, fluid shapes, hand-drawn warmth. Nature meets wisdom.

Typography

Libre Baskerville (display) + DM Sans (body) + Caveat (accent)

Best For

Women 25-45. Yoga/meditation community. Instagram/Pinterest marketing.

Mood

Warm. Human. Grounded. A sunlit apothecary or yoga studio.

Style 04 — Editorial Brutalist

Theme

Magazine layout, asymmetric grids, visible borders. The New Yorker style.

Typography

Instrument Serif (display) + Inter (body) + IBM Plex Mono (UI)

Best For

Content/SEO strategy. Building authority. Educated audience 28-50.

Mood

Authoritative. Intellectual. Sunday morning with coffee and a magazine.

Style 05 — Neo-Futurism

Theme

Neon accents, grid overlays, glitch effects. Cyberpunk meets spiritual.

Typography

Orbitron (display) + Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (data)

Best For

Gen-Z/Millennial 18-35. TikTok viral growth. AI-tech positioning.

Mood

Electric. Daring. Like stepping into a sci-fi movie interface.
